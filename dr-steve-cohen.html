About Dr. Cohen

Dr. Steve Cohen is the founder of HR Solutions On Call, an HR consulting firm in Lee’s Summit, Missouri, that acts as the on-call HR department for organizations that don’t have one. He is known as a “mess management” expert: the person companies call for harassment and discrimination complaints, workplace investigations, toxic employees, risky terminations, EEOC claims, and leadership teams in conflict.

He has worked with small businesses, nonprofits, ministries, cities, counties and health departments with anywhere from 2 to 17,000 employees, across health care, banking, manufacturing, government, education and service industries. He often steps in as interim HR director, and he has served as an expert witness on HR practices. Before consulting full time he was Vice President of Human Resources at Retirement Centers of America and Director of Human Resources at Santa Fe Healthcare.

Steve is also an educator. He served as Dean of the School of Professional & Graduate Studies at Baker University and as Interim Director of Affirmative Action at the University of Missouri–Kansas City, and he has taught as an adjunct professor at Northern Illinois University, Rockhurst University, Baker University, UMKC and National College.
