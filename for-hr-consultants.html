Questions from consultants

What does the Consultant plan cost?

$197/month, flat, with unlimited client workspaces. There is no per-client or per-employee fee. It starts with the same 7-day free trial as every plan, and you can cancel anytime.

How do client workspaces work?

Add each client once with their state, headcount, and industry. Pick a client from the Client box at the top of the sidebar and Your HR Coach answers using that client's state and company size. New chats are saved under that client, and the Template Library and Case Tracker fill in the client's details. Choose "All conversations" to step out.

Will it get state law right for clients in different states?

Each answer uses the selected client's state. For state-specific questions the coach checks official government sources live and links what it used, backed by a 50-state employment law library. The monthly law update email covers every state your clients are in, plus federal changes.

I'm already on the $99 plan. Can I upgrade?

Yes. Upgrade in one click from inside the app; billing is prorated.

Does this replace my judgment?

No. It gives HR guidance, not legal advice. Consultants use it to double-check an approach, draft documents faster, and work through complicated situations, so they can spend more time advising clients.
