Can I use ChatGPT for HR questions?

Yes, and for some tasks it's excellent. It can explain what FMLA is, tighten the wording of a policy, summarize a long email thread, or help you think through how to phrase a hard conversation. If you understand HR well and mainly want writing help, a general assistant may be all you need.

The risk is the questions where the answer depends on details you didn't think to mention — and in HR, that's most of them.

Why are general chatbots risky for HR decisions?

  1. The answer depends on the prompt. Ask "Can I fire an employee for being late?" and you'll get a generic at-will explanation. It won't necessarily ask whether that employee just filed a complaint, requested an accommodation, or is on protected leave — the facts that actually decide your risk.
  2. State law varies. Final paycheck deadlines, vacation payout, meal breaks, and paid sick leave differ by state and sometimes by city. A general answer can be correct in one state and wrong in yours.
  3. Confident errors. Large language models can produce plausible but incorrect statements. OpenAI itself warns that ChatGPT can make mistakes and advises checking important information.
  4. No HR judgment about what's missing. An experienced HR advisor's value is often spotting the issue you didn't raise: "Before you fire her — did she mention a medical condition?"

Example: the same question, two approaches

Question: "An employee has been late six times this month. Can I fire her?"

A typical general-chatbot answer explains at-will employment, suggests reviewing your attendance policy, and recommends documentation — accurate, but generic.

The HR-coach approach starts with the questions that change the answer: What state? Has she been warned in writing before? Is the lateness connected to a medical issue, childcare, or a recent schedule change? Are other employees with similar attendance treated the same way? Then it lays out next steps — often a written warning with a clear expectation rather than an immediate firing — and drafts the document.
