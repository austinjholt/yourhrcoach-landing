Flat pricing vs pricing that grows with your headcount

AllMyHR's Business tier starts at the same $99/month for 1-25 employees, then scales up as you hire. Your HR Coach stays at $99/month regardless of headcount. If you are at 10 employees today and 40 next year, the difference shows up on the invoice.

One named expert vs a pool of advisors

With AllMyHR you get access to a team of over 50 HR advisors. You do not know in advance who picks up. Your HR Coach is built on one person's body of work: Dr. Steve Cohen, founder of HR Solutions On Call, 40+ years of consulting and 150+ publications on HR and management. Every answer comes from the same playbook, so guidance does not change depending on who you reach.

Instant answers vs a callback

AllMyHR's model is a phone callback, usually inside about 10 minutes during business hours. That is fast for a human service. But HR crises happen on Friday afternoons, over the weekend, and in the middle of your busiest season. Your HR Coach answers at 11pm on a Sunday, and it writes the document while you are there.

Where AllMyHR may be the better fit: If you want a human voice on the phone and a 30-day trial to evaluate it, AllMyHR's advisor callback model gives you that; Your HR Coach is AI-first with the consulting firm available separately.

Common questions

Is AllMyHR cheaper than Your HR Coach?

At the entry level they match: both are $99/month for a small team. AllMyHR scales by employee count, so the more you hire the more you pay; Your HR Coach stays flat. AllMyHR also offers a $24.95/month Professional tier aimed at solo HR practitioners, not business owners.

Does Your HR Coach give me a person to call?

Not as part of the $99/month. You get instant AI guidance built on Dr. Cohen's expertise, 24/7. If a situation needs a human — an investigation, a union matter, litigation support — HR Solutions On Call, Dr. Cohen's consulting firm, handles that work directly.

Which one should I pick?

If talking to a person on the phone matters more to you than speed and cost predictability, AllMyHR is a reasonable choice. If you want an answer and a drafted document in the moment, at a price that does not move when you hire, start the free trial here.
