YourHRCoach.ai vs Gusto HR

Short answer: Gusto and YourHRCoach.ai do different jobs. Gusto is a payroll, benefits, and HR administration platform, with access to HR experts on its Premium plan. YourHRCoach.ai is an AI HR coach that tells you how to handle specific people situations and drafts the documents. It doesn't run payroll, so most businesses use it alongside Gusto rather than instead of it.

Is Gusto an HR platform or a payroll platform?

Both, but payroll is the foundation. Gusto handles running payroll, filing payroll taxes, new-hire paperwork, benefits administration, PTO tracking, onboarding checklists, and hiring tools, with features expanding at higher plan tiers. It's built to make the administrative side of employing people accurate and efficient.

What Gusto's software doesn't do by itself is tell you how to handle the human situations: the employee who keeps missing deadlines, the harassment complaint that landed on your desk this morning, or whether you can terminate someone who just returned from leave. For that, Gusto's answer is its HR experts, available on the Premium plan.

Does Gusto include HR advice?

According to Gusto, its Premium plan includes access to experienced HR professionals, including SHRM- and HRCI-certified experts, along with HR Resources such as templates and guidance. Gusto's help center notes that the HR Resources add-on for lower plans is a legacy option; new customers who want HR Resources now need the Premium plan. Lower plans (Simple and Plus) focus on payroll and core HR tools.

So if you're on Simple or Plus and want HR guidance, you have two paths: upgrade to Premium, or add a separate guidance tool.

How much does each cost?

Gusto's published pricing (October 2026) is a monthly base fee plus a per-person fee:

  • Simple: $49/month + $6 per person
  • Plus: $80/month + $12 per person
  • Premium: $180/month + $22 per person

For example, at 15 employees, list price works out to roughly $139/month on Simple, $260 on Plus, and $510 on Premium, before any add-ons. (Check Gusto's pricing page; your actual price may differ.)

YourHRCoach.ai is $99/month or $997/year, the same at 3 employees or 80. It doesn't replace your payroll provider, so the realistic comparison for a Gusto Simple or Plus customer is: upgrade to Premium for human HR experts plus Premium's other features, or keep your current plan and add YourHRCoach.ai for instant guidance and documents.

When does it make sense to use both?

  1. You need payroll done right — that's Gusto (or another payroll provider).
  2. You need to know what to do about a specific employee, today — that's YourHRCoach.ai. Ask at 9 p.m. on a Sunday and get step-by-step guidance plus a draft written warning or termination letter.
  3. You want a flat, predictable HR guidance cost that doesn't rise with every hire.

The two don't overlap much. Gusto keeps the records and the money flowing; YourHRCoach.ai helps you make and document the people decisions.

What kinds of questions is YourHRCoach.ai built for?

  • "Is my office manager exempt from overtime?"
  • "Write a job description for a part-time bookkeeper."
  • "Review my attendance policy and tell me what's missing."

Where Gusto Premium may be the better fit: If you want payroll, benefits, and access to certified human HR experts in one bill from one vendor — and Premium's per-person pricing works for your headcount — upgrading within Gusto keeps everything in one place. YourHRCoach.ai doesn't run payroll or administer benefits.

The bottom line

Gusto isn't a competitor to YourHRCoach.ai so much as a neighbor. If you need payroll, use Gusto or a similar provider. If you also need fast, expert-informed answers on employee situations without paying per head for them, YourHRCoach.ai is the guidance layer that sits on top. Frequently asked questions

Does YourHRCoach.ai replace Gusto?

No. YourHRCoach.ai doesn't run payroll, file payroll taxes, or administer benefits. It provides HR guidance and generates HR documents, so it's designed to work alongside Gusto or any other payroll provider.

Does Gusto give HR advice?

Gusto says its Premium plan includes access to certified HR experts and HR Resources. Its Simple and Plus plans focus on payroll and core HR tools; the HR Resources add-on for those plans is a legacy option.

How much does Gusto cost in 2026?

As listed on Gusto's pricing page in October 2026: Simple is $49/month plus $6 per person, Plus is $80/month plus $12 per person, and Premium is $180/month plus $22 per person. Confirm current pricing at gusto.com.

Does YourHRCoach.ai integrate with Gusto?

There is no direct integration. None is needed for the core use: you ask HR questions and generate documents in YourHRCoach.ai, and keep running payroll and employee records in Gusto.

Is YourHRCoach.ai cheaper than upgrading to Gusto Premium?

For most teams, yes, on HR guidance alone. YourHRCoach.ai is a flat $99/month or $997/year. Gusto Premium is priced per person and includes additional payroll and HR features, so compare based on everything you'd use.

Who should choose Gusto Premium instead?

Businesses that want payroll, benefits, and human HR experts from a single vendor, and that will use Premium's other features, may prefer upgrading within Gusto.

Is either tool a substitute for an employment attorney?

No. Both provide HR information and support, not legal advice. Use an employment attorney for lawsuits, agency charges, severance agreements, and other high-risk matters.
