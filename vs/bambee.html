Do it now vs wait for your HR manager

Bambee's value is a person assigned to your business who builds your policies with you. That is genuinely useful — and it runs at human speed. Your HR Coach is for the Friday-afternoon version of the problem: you need to know how to handle the termination and you need the letter today.

Bambee spans HR management, onboarding, and related administration. Your HR Coach does one thing: expert HR guidance and documents. If you want a platform to run HR operations, Bambee is the wider product. If you want the answer and the paperwork without adding a system to your business, that is this.
