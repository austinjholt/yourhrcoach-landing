What is Your HR Coach?

Your HR Coach is an AI-powered HR guidance tool built on Dr. Steve Cohen's 40+ years of HR consulting expertise. It provides instant answers to HR questions, generates professional documents (termination letters, employee handbooks, job descriptions), and helps small businesses stay compliant — all for $99/month with a free 7-day trial.

Is this just ChatGPT with a wrapper?

No. General AI tools answer from whatever is on the internet. Your HR Coach answers from Dr. Cohen's own body of HR work — 40 years of consulting and 150+ publications — loaded into the system so the guidance reflects how an experienced HR consultant would actually handle the situation, step by step, and in the order things need to happen.

Is HR guidance the same as legal advice?

No. Your HR Coach gives HR guidance, not legal advice, and it is not a substitute for your attorney. It tells you how to handle the situation the way an experienced HR professional would, and it will tell you when something is serious enough that you should get an employment attorney involved.

Does it cover my specific state's laws?

Employment law varies by state and changes regularly. Your HR Coach covers federal law (FLSA, OSHA, Title VII, ADA, FMLA) and will flag where your state commonly differs — final paycheck timing, at-will exceptions, required notices — so you know what to verify before you act. Tell it which state you are in when you ask.

What if I need help beyond what the AI can answer?

Your HR Coach is backed by HR Solutions On Call, Dr. Cohen's consulting firm. If a situation needs a human — a live investigation, a union matter, litigation support — you can bring in the firm directly at oncallhrgroup.com.

How much does HR help cost for a small business?

Traditional HR consultants charge $150-300/hour. PEOs cost $100-200/employee/month. Your HR Coach provides expert HR guidance for $99/month (or $997/year) — accessible 24/7 with no per-question fees. Start with a free 7-day trial.

Do I need HR if I have fewer than 50 employees?

Yes. Federal employment laws like FLSA, OSHA, and IRCA apply from your very first hire. At 15 employees, Title VII and ADA kick in. The risk of not having HR guidance isn't the size of your team — it's the cost of a single mistake. A wrongful termination lawsuit averages over $200,000.
